A fighter takes off from a runway in perspective, shoots down the waves of enemy planes, comes back and lands; the fuel runs down and what is left is paid in points. Sixteen stages. The title comes from running the ROM, checked against openMSX; the game in GIF, played in openMSX by a pilot that takes off, fights and lands; the tunes, from its own engine; the four that play, identical to openMSX frame by frame.

Casio - (c) 1985 - 24 KB cartridge (header at 0x8000) - MSX - Dump d916f0e4...

There are no screenshots of the game here. The title comes from running the ROM (checked against openMSX) and the GIFs are the ROM running in openMSX: on every frame the VRAM, the RAM and the VDP registers are logged, and our own code paints them.

What is inside

The game

  • The game: the title, the controls, take-off, combat, landing, fuel, the lives and the points.
  • The stages: the sixteen stages, the waves, the enemies and how they move.
  • The sprites: the plane, the enemies, the shots and the explosions put together from the ROM tables, frame by frame.
  • The texts: every text in the ROM, with its translation.
  • The music: the five tunes, from its own sound engine and checked against openMSX.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: SCORE inside HISCORE, code nobody calls, a tune nobody asks for, the stick plus the cursors.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the cartridge, the BIOS it uses, the main loop, the RAM and the tools.
